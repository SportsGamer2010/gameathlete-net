About

Start With What You Love

GameAthlete is a hybrid of esports community, technology academy, project lab, workforce-development organization, and talent marketplace.

Our mission

Gaming is the entry point. Technology skills, verified projects, mentoring, and community are the bridge. Career opportunity is the destination—not a guarantee, but a pathway worth building toward.

Founded by Brian Burnett, GameAthlete serves learners, gamers, parents, employers, workforce partners, and project partners—with age-aware privacy for youth participants.
