Freelance

Platforms learners may explore include Upwork and Fiverr for client work and portfolio building. These are external marketplaces—not GameAthlete partners unless explicitly verified.

Field Services

Field Nation and WorkMarket are examples of field-service marketplaces learners may explore for on-site technology work. Listed as potential pathways only.

Apprenticeship

Structured earn-and-learn programs through workforce partners. GameAthlete prepares candidates; placement depends on partner programs and employer needs.

Employment

Employer partners and staffing relationships may connect verified talent to internships, contracts, and full-time roles through the Hire workflow.

Entrepreneurship

Freelance services, local tech support, web development businesses, automation consulting, and technology contracting—building on GameAthlete Works project experience.
