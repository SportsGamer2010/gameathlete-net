How the program works

GameAthlete meets students where they are. Some learn by watching, some by building, some by competing. Gaming is the common language—we connect that energy to technology, leadership, and career skills through live platforms like 2KCompLeague.com.

Leagues stay free. Academy pathways, coaching, and memberships are available when you're ready—never a requirement to play.
