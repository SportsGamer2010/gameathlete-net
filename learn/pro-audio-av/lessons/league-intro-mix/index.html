Produce the League Intro Mix

Combine hype audio, voiceover, and game SFX into a 60-second broadcast opener.

Game scenario: Friday Night Fights needs a professional intro that plays before the first match—like a TV broadcast cold open.

Steps

  1. Write the 60-second script

    Draft voiceover copy: league name, tonight's featured matchup, sponsor shout-out placeholder. Read it aloud—aim for 45 seconds of speech with 15 seconds of music/SFX padding.

  2. Record voiceover

    Use your phone or USB mic in a quiet room. Record 3 takes. Pick the clearest one and remove breath pops in Audacity.

  3. Layer the mix

    Track 1: background bed (low volume royalty-free loop). Track 2: voiceover. Track 3: hype sting hit at the 50-second mark leading into 'Game 1 starting now.'

  4. Master and deliver

    Normalize the final mix to -3 dB. Export as league-intro-mix.wav. Write a cue sheet: 'Play at stream start, fade under casters at 0:58.'

Your goal

60-second League Intro Mix

Broadcast opener with voiceover, music bed, and hype sting finale.

Format: WAV (~60 seconds) + cue sheet

Success criteria

Check each item when your deliverable meets the standard.

Tools

  • Audacity
  • Microphone
  • OBS
  • Royalty-free music loop
