Learning connection

How This Tool Was Built

Expand each topic to see how gaming connects to real technology skills.

How video frames are analyzed

The pipeline samples strategically, prioritizes post-fight screens, and accepts timestamps when automation is unsure.

How OCR reads game statistics

OCR and optional vision models turn scorecard and punch-stat pixels into structured numbers for review.

How scorecards become database records

Detected values are normalized, confidence-flagged, corrected by users, then saved as a report.

How APIs connect websites

GameAthlete APIs create jobs, update status, export data, and can submit confirmed results to Esports Boxing League.

How developers test accuracy

Fixtures and confidence thresholds validate parsing without reprocessing live YouTube videos in every test.

How data is converted into reports

Derived stats are calculated only from detected fields. Missing values stay blank.

How privacy and platform rules affect product design

Reports are private by default. Private videos are not accessed. League submission never skips moderation.

Privacy

What we process

Reports are private by default unless you explicitly share or publish them.

  • Public YouTube metadata (title, channel, thumbnail) when available
  • Screenshots you upload for scorecard and punch-stat extraction
  • Temporary analysis frames and logs tied to your job
  • Optional league submission only after you confirm the report
  • You can keep reports private; shared links are opt-in
